# Upgrade the Internal Flash Drive
From the perspective of the year 2025, the available memory of the HP T420 is generally lacking by most current requirements, both for RAM and for hard drive space. Despite many applications suggesting a minimum of 8GB of RAM, the HP T420 contains a total of 2GB of RAM and it is soldered on, so no easy upgrade path exists. The "harddrive" is implemented via an internal USB 3.0 flash drive, which can be upgraded relatively easily. While the HP T420 typically contains an 8GB flash drive, an upgrade to a 128GB flash drive has had no noticeable issues so far.

## Prepare the New Flash Drive
- Purchase a typical USB 3.0 flash drive from any of a variety of warehouse sized stores.  In 2025, 128GB versions from SanDisk are readily available.  Other brands of USB flash drive are also available.
- Carefully remove the plastic covering from the store-bought USB stick.  This is a destructive process but be gentle.  The internal circuitry needs to remain unharmed, so controlled demolition is required.  When the plastic cover is on, the USB stick will not fit in the internal space.  A demonstration can be found in (Reference 1), see below.

## Open the Case
The following are instructions from the manual:
- Lay the unit flat on a stable surface with the right side up.
- Remove the two screws from the back I/O panel.
- Slide the access panel approximately 6 mm (.24 in) toward the back of the chassis, and then lift the panel off of the thin client.
Not mentioned in the manual is that the sliding the access panel requires significant downward pressure on the access panel while sliding toward the back.  A quick firm motion works best, which essentially requires the rubber feet to be present on the bottom if working on a typical solid flat tabletop.  These addition insights can be found in (Reference 1), see below.

## Swap the Flash Drives
There is a restraining mechanism that keeps the USB flash drive in place.  The restraint has to be released before the original flash drive can be removed, so do not just yank.  Once the original flash drive is gently and safely removed, install the new flash drive and reengage the restraining mechanism.  

Close the case by sliding the access panel back into place and reinstalling the screws.

## Hardware Specifications of HP T420
Information from the manual:
- AMD GX-209JA System-on-Chip (SOC), including a 1.0 GHz dual core APU and discrete-level GPU.
- Integrated 2 GB DDR3L SDRAM system memory; 1,066 MT/s data transfer rate.
- 8 GB or 16 GB capacity flash memory storage on internal Super Speed USB 3.0 modules.
- Integrated Gigabit Ethernet LAN module; rear access RJ-45 modular jack.
- One VGA analog video output and one DVI-D digital video output.
- Two Hi-Speed USB 2.0 front access ports and two Hi-Speed USB 2.0 rear access ports.
- Front access headset jack: This connector supports audio out (speakers and headphones) or a headset (with headphones and a microphone).
